Interface Redesign (2026)

For my final apprenticeship project (IPA), I redesigned an internal customer platform that had become outdated — both visually and technically. I took the project from research to finished mockups entirely on my own: defining the target audience, analyzing competitors, restructuring the information architecture, and delivering a complete visual redesign. It was my first experience running a full design project under real time pressure and it taught me a lot about workflow, time management and the discipline of seeing something through from brief to delivery.
