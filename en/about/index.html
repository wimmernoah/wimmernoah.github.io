Noah Wimmer DE

Who

I'm Noah, a young creative from Switzerland. After finishing school I started my apprenticeship as a Mediamatiker, which I completed in 2026. Outside of work I'm someone with a lot of goals — always looking to improve, but also knowing when to slow down and enjoy time with the people around me. I have a passion for good design, both digital and analog, and a soft spot for fashion, music and sports.

How

I work with precision and don't settle for results I'm not proud of. I like to start from scratch — no templates, no shortcuts. I design from the user's perspective, with references for inspiration but never as a crutch. I value timelessness over trends, clarity over noise and intentional design over gimmicks. I obsess over details — sometimes too much. I enjoy working in a team, but I'm just as comfortable working independently.

Why

I do this because I believe good design genuinely changes things — how people feel, how they interact, how they see the world. I'm driven by what's possible, inspired by people like Steve Jobs, Dieter Rams and Pharrell Williams who proved that vision and craft together can move culture. I know what I'm capable of and I'm looking for a place to prove it.

Contact

Are you hiring, looking for a collaborator or just curious to know more? Whether you want to talk about a project, get to know me better or simply have a question — I'm always open for a conversation. Let's have a Chat.
